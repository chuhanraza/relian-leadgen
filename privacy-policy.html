Privacy Policy

Relian MFG · Last updated August 5, 2026

This policy explains how Relian MFG uses the Gmail API in connection with its business email account. It applies to that specific integration, not to relianmfg.com as a whole.

What we access

Relian MFG's automated outreach tooling connects to a single Gmail account (relianmfg@gmail.com) using the Gmail API's gmail.compose scope. This scope permits the application to create, view, and manage draft emails on that account — nothing more.

What we do with it

  • The application composes outbound business emails (e.g. sourcing inquiries to prospective customers) and saves them as drafts in the Gmail account's Drafts folder.
  • No draft is ever sent automatically. A human — the account holder — reviews every draft in the Gmail interface and decides whether to send, edit, or discard it.
  • The application does not send email on its own; sending happens only when the account holder clicks Send in Gmail itself.

What we do not do

  • We do not read, search, or access the account's inbox, sent mail, or any existing correspondence.
  • We do not store the content of any email, draft, or message outside of the Gmail account itself. The application holds no local or database copy of email bodies.
  • We do not share, sell, or transmit any Gmail data to third parties.
  • We do not use Gmail data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to composing the outreach drafts described above.

Data retention

Drafts created by the application live only inside the Gmail account, governed by Google's own retention and deletion controls. The application itself retains no copy of email content once a draft is created. Records the application does keep (e.g. which company was contacted and on what date) are limited to that operational metadata — not email content — and are stored in Relian MFG's private internal database, not shared externally.

Third-party access

The only third party involved is Google, as the provider of the Gmail API itself. No other third party is granted access to this Gmail account or its data. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The account holder can revoke this application's access at any time via Google Account → Security → Third-party access. Revoking access immediately stops the application from creating further drafts.
